# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive; harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in the repository's security policy ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation of anyone who violates this code.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, including enforcement guidelines, applies and is incorporated by reference.
